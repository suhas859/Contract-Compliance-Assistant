# Software License and Support Agreement
Contract ID: CTR-2026-0071
Supplier: Larkspur Analytics Inc.
Tax ID: 88-4471029
Contract Owner: H. Talbi, Procurement
Total Contract Value: $142,800 USD
Contract Validity Period: 2026-03-15 to 2027-03-14
Vendor Risk Tier: Tier 2 — High (per POL-VRM-005)

## 1. License Grant
Larkspur Analytics Inc. grants the Company a non-exclusive, non-transferable
license to use the Larkspur Insights Platform for up to 250 named users
across the Company's Finance and Operations departments, for the duration
of this Agreement.

## 2. Fees
Annual license fee: $120,000, invoiced upfront upon execution. Annual
support and maintenance fee: $22,800 (19% of license fee), invoiced upfront
alongside the license fee. Total first-year contract value: $142,800.

## 3. Support Terms
Vendor shall provide Business Hours support (8am-6pm ET, Monday-Friday)
with a 4-hour response time for Severity 1 issues and 1-business-day
response for Severity 2 and below. Vendor shall provide quarterly platform
updates and shall notify the Company at least 30 days in advance of any
update requiring user retraining.

## 4. Data Privacy and Confidentiality
As the platform will process Company financial and operational data
classified as Confidential, Vendor shall: restrict data use solely to
providing the licensed services; retain Company data only for the duration
of the Agreement plus 30 days for transition purposes; notify the Company
of any breach within 72 hours of discovery; comply with applicable data
protection regulations; and permit the Company to audit data handling
practices with 15 days notice, consistent with Vendor's Tier 2 status under
POL-VRM-005.

## 5. Service Level Commitment
Vendor commits to 99.5% platform uptime measured monthly, excluding
scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 5 business days in
advance. Failure to meet this commitment in any given month entitles the
Company to a service credit of 5% of the monthly-equivalent license fee
per 0.5% of uptime shortfall, up to a maximum of 25% credit in any month.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate for convenience with 60 days written notice.
Either party may terminate for cause with 15 days written notice and an
opportunity to cure. Upon termination, the Company shall receive a pro-rated
refund of any prepaid, unused license fees.

## 7. Liability and Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify the Company against third-party claims arising from
intellectual property infringement related to the licensed platform.
Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at total fees paid in
the preceding 12 months, except for breaches of Section 4 (Data Privacy).

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the
Company's headquarters is located.
