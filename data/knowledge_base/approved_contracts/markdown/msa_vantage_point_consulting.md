# Master Services Agreement
Contract ID: CTR-2026-0055
Supplier: Vantage Point Consulting Group LLC
Tax ID: 45-8812093
Contract Owner: P. Dubois, VP of Procurement
Total Contract Value: $875,000 USD
Contract Validity Period: 2026-01-15 to 2029-01-14
Vendor Risk Tier: Tier 1 — Critical (per POL-VRM-005)

## 1. Scope of Work
Vantage Point Consulting Group LLC ("Vendor") shall provide enterprise
digital transformation advisory services, including business process
reengineering, technology roadmap development, change management support,
and program governance for the Company's Finance and Operations
modernization initiative, as further detailed in Exhibits A through D.
Services shall be delivered across three phases: Discovery (Months 1-3),
Design (Months 4-9), and Implementation Support (Months 10-24).

## 2. Term and Renewal
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months
unless terminated earlier per Section 9. The Agreement may be renewed for
successive 12-month terms upon mutual written agreement, subject to the
renewal review requirements of SOP-CR-012, including reconfirmation of
Vendor's risk tier status and reapplication of then-current Procurement
Policy approval thresholds.

## 3. Payment Terms
### 3.1 Fee Structure
Total not-to-exceed contract value is $875,000, allocated as follows:
- Discovery Phase: $150,000, invoiced in two equal installments upon
  kickoff and phase completion
- Design Phase: $350,000, invoiced monthly at $58,333.33 based on
  percentage-of-completion against the agreed project plan
- Implementation Support Phase: $375,000, invoiced monthly at $26,785.71
  over 14 months

### 3.2 Payment Schedule
All invoices are due Net 30 from receipt. No advance payment is authorized
under this Agreement. Invoices must include time and expense detail
sufficient for Company's Finance team to reconcile against the phase
budget above.

### 3.3 Expense Reimbursement
Pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses shall be reimbursed at cost,
capped at 8% of the applicable phase fee, and must be submitted with
receipts within 60 days of being incurred.

## 4. Deliverables
1. Current-state assessment report (end of Discovery)
2. Future-state process design documentation (end of Design)
3. Technology roadmap and implementation plan (end of Design)
4. Monthly implementation status reports (Implementation Support phase)
5. Change management training materials and delivery
6. Final program closeout report

## 5. Key Personnel
Vendor shall assign a named Engagement Lead and Program Manager for the
duration of the engagement. Substitution of either role requires Company's
prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, and Vendor must
provide a minimum 2-week transition/knowledge-transfer period for any
approved substitution.

## 6. Vendor Risk and Security Requirements
As a Tier 1 vendor under the Vendor Risk Management Policy (POL-VRM-005),
Vendor shall:
1. Maintain a current SOC 2 Type II report, provided to the Company
   annually and upon request
2. Participate in an annual security audit conducted by or on behalf of
   the Company
3. Maintain a documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan,
   reviewed with the Company annually
4. Notify the Company of any actual or suspected security incident
   affecting Company data within 24 hours of discovery — a shorter window
   than the standard 72-hour requirement in POL-SEC-002, reflecting this
   engagement's elevated risk tier
5. Disclose all subcontractors granted access to Company data prior to
   granting such access, per POL-VRM-005 Section 5

## 7. Data Privacy and Confidentiality
### 7.1 Data Handling
Vendor shall access Company data solely to perform the Services and shall
not use, disclose, or retain such data for any other purpose. Data shall be
classified per the Company's data classification scheme, and Vendor shall
apply security controls appropriate to the highest classification level of
data accessed.

### 7.2 Retention and Destruction
Upon completion of each phase, Vendor shall purge working data no longer
required for the current phase. Upon termination or expiration of this
Agreement, Vendor shall return or certify destruction of all Company data
within 30 days, consistent with POL-VRM-005 Section 7.

### 7.3 Breach Notification
Vendor shall notify the Company within 24 hours of discovering any breach
or suspected breach involving Company data, and shall cooperate fully with
the Company's incident response process, including providing all relevant
logs and forensic information upon request.

### 7.4 Regulatory Compliance
Vendor shall comply with all applicable data protection regulations
including GDPR and CCPA where the underlying data subjects fall within
those regulations' scope, and shall support the Company's response to any
data subject access requests within 5 business days of request.

### 7.5 Audit Rights
The Company, or its designated third-party auditor, may audit Vendor's data
handling and security practices no more than twice per 12-month period upon
15 days written notice, or immediately following a security incident.

## 8. Confidentiality
Both parties shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed
under this Agreement, consistent with the Confidentiality Policy
(POL-CONF-004), for a period of 3 years following termination or
expiration of this Agreement.

## 9. Termination
### 9.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days
written notice, given the scale and multi-phase nature of the engagement.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other
party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days
of written notice of the breach.

### 9.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Vendor shall be paid for Services satisfactorily
performed through the termination date, and shall comply with the data
return/destruction obligations in Section 7.2.

## 10. Liability and Indemnification
### 10.1 Limitation of Liability
Except for breaches of Section 7 (Data Privacy and Confidentiality) or
Section 8 (Confidentiality), or claims arising from gross negligence or
willful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement
shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding
the claim.

### 10.2 Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company from any
third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement,
negligence, or willful misconduct in the performance of Services.

## 11. Insurance
Vendor shall maintain insurance coverage consistent with Tier 1
requirements under POL-VRM-005 Section 6, and shall provide certificates
of insurance to the Company prior to commencement of Services and upon
each policy renewal.

## 12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the
Company's headquarters is located. Any disputes arising under this
Agreement shall first be escalated to each party's designated executive
sponsor for good-faith resolution before either party pursues formal
dispute resolution.

## 13. Miscellaneous
This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes
all prior negotiations or agreements. Amendments must be in writing and
signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Neither party may
assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except in
connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.
