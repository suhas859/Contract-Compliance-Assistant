and Pinegrove Design Studio for the purpose of evaluating a potential
branding partnership. Both parties agree to keep any shared business,
design, or financial information confidential for the duration of this
agreement plus 2 years following termination. Either party may terminate
with 10 days written notice. This agreement does not obligate either party
to enter into a further business relationship.
