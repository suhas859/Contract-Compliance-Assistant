# Supplier Agreement
Contract ID: CTR-2026-0088
Supplier: Acme Logistics Inc.
Tax ID: 94-3021177
Contract Owner: J. Ramisetti, Procurement
Total Contract Value: $185,000 USD
Contract Validity Period: 2026-02-01 to 2027-01-31

## 1. Scope of Work
Acme Logistics Inc. shall provide warehousing and last-mile distribution
services for the Northeast region, including inventory storage, order
fulfillment, and delivery tracking, as detailed in Exhibit A.

## 2. Payment Terms
Payment shall be made monthly in arrears, Net 30 from invoice date. Monthly
service fee: $15,416.67. Total contract value shall not be exceeded without
a signed amendment.

## 3. Deliverables
- Weekly inventory accuracy report
- Monthly on-time delivery rate report (target: 97%)
- Quarterly business review meeting

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience with 30 days
written notice. Termination for cause (material breach) requires 15 days
written notice and an opportunity to cure.

## 5. Liability and Indemnification
Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claims
arising from Supplier's negligence in performance of services under this
agreement, up to a cap of the total contract value.

## 6. Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the
Company's headquarters is located.
