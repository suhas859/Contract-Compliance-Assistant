# Knowledge Article: Procurement & Contract FAQ
Article ID: KA-0142
Category: Procurement
Last Updated: 2025-11-02

## Q: What is the approval threshold for a new contract?
A: It depends on total contract value. Under $10,000 requires only a
Department Manager. Between $10,000 and $50,000 requires Director approval.
Between $50,000 and $250,000 requires VP of Procurement approval. Anything
over $250,000 requires both CFO approval and a mandatory Legal Review. See
Procurement Policy POL-PROC-001, Section 2.

## Q: Is advance payment allowed?
A: Generally no. Advance payment is only allowed for new suppliers (fewer
than 2 prior transactions) up to 20% of contract value, or with explicit
written approval from the VP of Procurement. See POL-PROC-001, Section 3.

## Q: Which policy governs contract termination?
A: Procurement Policy POL-PROC-001, Section 7. Standard terms are 15 days
notice for termination for cause, or 30 days for termination for
convenience, unless the contract negotiates a different period.

## Q: What clauses are mandatory in every contract?
A: Scope of work, payment terms, contract validity period, termination
conditions, a data privacy/confidentiality clause, and liability/
indemnification terms. See POL-PROC-001 Section 4 and POL-SEC-002 for the
specific data privacy clause requirements.

## Q: What happens if an invoice exceeds the contract value?
A: If it exceeds by more than 5% without an approved amendment on file, it
must be flagged and routed back to the contract owner. If it exceeds the
total contract value outright, this is a Fail, not a Warning, and payment
must be blocked pending an amendment. See POL-PROC-001 Section 5 and
Payment Policy POL-PAY-003 Section 5.

## Q: Does every contract need a legal review?
A: Only if it exceeds $250,000, involves third-party data sharing, or has
non-standard termination/liability terms. See SOP-CA-010 Step 3.
