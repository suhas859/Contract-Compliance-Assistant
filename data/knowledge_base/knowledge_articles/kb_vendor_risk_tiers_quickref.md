# Knowledge Article: Vendor Risk Tiers Quick Reference
Article ID: KA-0156
Category: Vendor Risk

Tier 1 = Critical (core systems/Restricted data) — annual audit + SOC 2
Type II required. Tier 2 = High — annual questionnaire + SOC 2 Type I.
Tier 3 = Moderate — questionnaire every 2 years. Tier 4 = Low — standard
onboarding only. Full detail: Vendor Risk Management Policy POL-VRM-005.
