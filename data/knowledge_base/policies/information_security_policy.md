# Information Security Policy
Document ID: POL-SEC-002
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2025-03-15
Owner: Information Security Office

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines minimum data protection and confidentiality requirements
that must be reflected in any contract where a third party may access,
process, store, or transmit company data.

## 2. Mandatory Data Privacy Clause
Any contract involving a supplier, vendor, or partner that will have access
to company data — including customer data, employee data, or confidential
business information — **must** include a data privacy clause covering:

1. Data ownership and permitted use restrictions
2. Data retention and secure deletion requirements at contract end
3. Breach notification timeline (must not exceed 72 hours from discovery)
4. Compliance with applicable data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA
   where applicable)
5. Right to audit the supplier's data handling practices

**Contracts that do not contain this clause are non-compliant and must be
rejected or returned for revision before approval, regardless of contract
value.**

## 3. Data Classification
All data shared with third parties under a contract must be classified
(Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) and the classification must
determine the required security controls, which should be referenced in the
contract's data privacy clause.

## 4. Subcontractor Restrictions
Suppliers may not share company data with subcontractors or downstream
vendors unless explicitly permitted in the contract and the subcontractor
agrees to equivalent data protection terms.

## 5. Enforcement
Contract reviewers must verify the presence of a compliant data privacy
clause during the Contract Review workflow. This is a mandatory check and
cannot be waived by a Department Manager — waivers require sign-off from the
Information Security Office.
