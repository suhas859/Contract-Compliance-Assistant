# Confidentiality Policy
Document ID: POL-CONF-004
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2025-05-01

All employees, contractors, and suppliers engaged under a Company agreement
must treat any non-public business information as confidential and may not
disclose it to third parties without prior written authorization from the
Company. This obligation survives termination of the underlying contract
for a period of 3 years.
