# Procurement Policy
Document ID: POL-PROC-001
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Owner: Procurement Department

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the procurement of goods and services, including contract
approval, supplier selection, and payment terms for all vendor agreements.

## 2. Approval Thresholds
| Contract Value (USD) | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | Department Manager |
| $10,000 - $50,000 | Director |
| $50,000 - $250,000 | VP of Procurement |
| Over $250,000 | Chief Financial Officer + Legal Review |

Any contract exceeding $250,000 requires a mandatory legal review prior to
signature. Contracts cannot be executed without evidence of the required
approval level being obtained.

## 3. Advance Payment
Advance payment to suppliers is **not permitted** except under the following
conditions:
- The supplier is a new vendor with fewer than 2 prior transactions and the
  advance does not exceed 20% of total contract value, OR
- Advance payment is explicitly approved in writing by the VP of Procurement.

All other payment must follow standard net-30 terms from invoice receipt,
unless otherwise negotiated and documented in the contract's payment schedule.

## 4. Mandatory Contract Clauses
Every supplier contract must include, at minimum:
1. Scope of work / deliverables
2. Payment terms and schedule
3. Contract validity period (start and end dates)
4. Termination conditions
5. Data privacy and confidentiality clause (see Information Security Policy
   POL-SEC-002 for required language)
6. Liability and indemnification terms

Contracts missing any of the above are considered **non-compliant** and must
be flagged for revision before approval.

## 5. Invoice Tolerance
Invoices submitted against an active contract must not exceed the approved
contract value by more than **5%** without a formal contract amendment on
file. Invoices exceeding this tolerance must be flagged as a compliance
warning and routed back to the contract owner for review.

## 6. Supplier Verification
All invoices must be validated against the supplier name and tax ID recorded
in the original contract. Mismatches must be treated as a compliance failure,
not a warning, and escalated immediately.

## 7. Contract Termination
Contracts may be terminated for cause (breach, non-performance) with 15 days
written notice, or for convenience with 30 days written notice, unless a
different notice period is explicitly negotiated in the contract.
