# SOP: Contract Approval Process
Document ID: SOP-CA-010
Version: 1.2
Effective Date: 2025-02-01
Owner: Legal & Procurement

## Purpose
Standardizes the steps required to review and approve a new supplier
contract before execution.

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Intake
Contract owner submits the drafted contract along with supplier details and
estimated total contract value.

### Step 2 — Policy Compliance Check
Contract is checked against:
- Procurement Policy (POL-PROC-001) for approval thresholds and mandatory
  clauses
- Information Security Policy (POL-SEC-002) for data privacy clause
  requirements

Any missing mandatory clause must be logged as a **Fail** finding, not a
warning, since it blocks approval.

### Step 3 — Legal Review (if applicable)
Required whenever:
- Contract value exceeds $250,000, OR
- Contract involves data sharing with a third party, OR
- Contract includes non-standard termination or liability terms

Legal review omission for contracts requiring it is itself a critical
compliance failure and must be logged, even if all other clauses are
present.

### Step 4 — Approval Routing
Contract routed to the approver defined by the Procurement Policy threshold
table based on total contract value.

### Step 5 — Execution
Once approved, contract is signed and stored, and becomes the governing
document for all downstream validations (invoices, amendments).

## Common Failure Points (for reviewer reference)
- Missing data privacy clause (most frequent finding)
- Contract routed to wrong approval level for its value
- Legal review skipped for high-value contracts
- Payment schedule not clearly defined
