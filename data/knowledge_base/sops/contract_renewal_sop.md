# SOP: Contract Renewal
Document ID: SOP-CR-012
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2025-09-01

Contract owners must initiate renewal review at least 45 days before
contract expiration. Renewal requires reconfirming supplier standing (no
open Tier 1 risk findings), reapplying the current Procurement Policy
approval threshold based on updated contract value, and reissuing the data
privacy clause if the Information Security Policy has been revised since
original signature. Contracts not renewed or terminated by their expiration
date automatically lapse and require a new full approval cycle to reinstate.
