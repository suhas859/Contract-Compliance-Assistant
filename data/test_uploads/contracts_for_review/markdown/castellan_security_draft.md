## 1. Scope of Work
Castellan Security Integrations Inc. shall design, install, and maintain
physical security systems (badge access, CCTV, intrusion detection) across
three Company facilities, as detailed in Exhibit A.

## 2. Payment Terms
An initial payment of $164,800 (40% of total contract value) is due upon
signing to cover equipment procurement. Remaining balance paid in 18 equal
monthly installments of $13,733.33 following installation completion.

## 3. Deliverables
- Facility security system design documentation
- Installation completion certification per site
- Quarterly system maintenance and testing report

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for convenience with 45 days written notice.

## 5. Data Privacy and Confidentiality
Castellan shall not use Company facility layout or security system data
for any purpose outside this agreement, shall notify the Company of any
data breach within 72 hours, and shall comply with applicable data
protection regulations. The Company reserves the right to audit data
handling practices.

## 6. Liability and Indemnification
Castellan shall indemnify the Company against claims arising from its
negligence, capped at total contract value.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the
Company's headquarters is located.
